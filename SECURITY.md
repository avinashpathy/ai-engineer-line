# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please **don't open a public issue** for security problems.

Email: **security@your-domain.example** (replace with your own security contact before publishing) with the subject line `SECURITY: AI Engineer Line`, or use GitHub's **Security → Report a vulnerability** (private advisory) on this repository.

Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and the impact you think it has. You'll get an acknowledgement within 5 working days. Please give a reasonable time to fix the issue before disclosing it publicly.

## Data deletion requests

To have your account and all your progress deleted, email the address above from the email address you signed in with.

## Scope

- In scope: this repository's code, the deployed site, and the Supabase policies in `supabase/migrations/`.
- Out of scope: denial-of-service testing, social engineering, and third-party services (GitHub, Google, Supabase, Vercel) themselves.
