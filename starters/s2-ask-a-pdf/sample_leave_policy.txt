Acme Learning Pvt Ltd - Leave Policy (sample document for practice; the company is fictional).
This policy applies to all full-time employees. Part-time employees receive leave in proportion to the days they work.
The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December.
--- page 2 ---
Casual leave. Every employee gets 12 days of casual leave each year. Casual leave cannot be carried forward; unused days lapse on 31 December.
Casual leave of more than 3 days in a row needs approval from your manager at least one week in advance.
--- page 3 ---
Sick leave. Employees get 10 days of paid sick leave each year. A medical certificate is required if you are on sick leave for more than 2 days in a row.
Unused sick leave can be carried forward, up to a maximum of 30 days.
--- page 4 ---
Earned leave. Employees earn 1.5 days of earned leave for every month worked, which is 18 days a year. Up to 45 days of earned leave can be carried forward.
Earned leave can be cashed out when you leave the company, at your basic salary rate.
--- page 5 ---
Work from home. Employees may work from home up to 2 days a week with their manager's approval.
Internet costs while working from home are reimbursed up to 1,000 rupees a month, against a bill.
